The Challenge

Humana's MarketPoint organization required an annual recertification program for approximately 90,000 sales agents, ensuring ongoing compliance with Medicare regulations while maintaining engagement, reducing learner fatigue, and minimizing operational disruptions.

The existing program contained recurring content, lengthy training experiences, and compressed update cycles that created challenges for both learners and stakeholders. The initiative required a scalable approach that balanced regulatory requirements, learner experience, stakeholder expectations, accessibility standards, and business performance outcomes.

The goal was to transform recertification from a compliance exercise into a streamlined, engaging, and data-informed enablement experience that improved adoption, reduced redundancy, and supported business readiness year after year.
