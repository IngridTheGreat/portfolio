Organizational Change Manager

Helping organizations navigate transformation through strategy, communication, adoption, and workforce readiness.

"Successful transformation happens when people understand the change, believe in the change, and have the capability to execute the change."

Change Management Capabilities

CHANGE STRATEGY

• developd change strategies that align people, processes, technology, and business objectives to support successful adoption of new workflows, systems, and organizational initiatives

• applied structured change management methodologies, including Prosci ADKAR principles, to assess impacts, define adoption strategies, and guide employees through transitions

• partnered with business leaders, SMEs, and cross-functional teams to create change roadmaps, communication plans, training strategies, and reinforcement activities that enable sustainable change

STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT

• built strong partnerships with stakeholders across business, technical, and operational teams to understand impacts, address concerns, and align change activities with organizational priorities

• facilitated collaboration between leaders, SMEs, trainers, and end users to ensure communication, training, and support resources address the needs of impacted audiences

• translated complex business, healthcare, insurance, and technology changes into clear messaging and actionable guidance that improves understanding, engagement, and adoption

CHANGE READINESS

• assessed workforce readiness by identifying knowledge gaps, process impacts, skill requirements, and adoption barriers that may affect successful implementation

• designated readiness and enablement strategies that combine communication, training, coaching, and performance support to prepare employees for new ways of working

• used feedback, learner insights, and performance data to identify adoption challenges and continuously improve change support strategies

Change Enablement Framework

AWARENESS

• communicated the business reasons, objectives, and impacts of change to help employees understand why the change is needed and how it supports organizational goals

• created clear messaging and communication strategies that connect the change to employee roles, workflows, and day-to-day responsibilities

DESIRE

• engaged stakeholders and employees to build commitment and motivation to participate in and support the change

• identified concerns, resistance points, and adoption barriers while developing strategies that encourage ownership and acceptance

KNOWLEDGE

• developed learning strategies and training solutions that provide employees with the knowledge needed to understand new processes, systems, and expectations

• created role-based learning resources, job aids, and performance support tools that enable employees to successfully transition to new ways of working

ABILITY

• enabled employees to apply new knowledge through practice, coaching, and hands-on support to build confidence and proficiency

• partnered with leaders, trainers, and SMEs to reinforce new behaviors, workflows, and technology adoption within daily operations

REINFORCEMENT

• measured adoption and performance outcomes through feedback, assessments, and analytics to identify opportunities for continued improvement

• developed reinforcement strategies that sustain new behaviors through ongoing support, communication, and continuous learning
