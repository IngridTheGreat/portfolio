Measurement and Data Capabilities

Data Analysis

• analyzed 38,000 survey responses, including 7,100 agent verbatims; categorized feedback to identify trends (e.g., “thank” mentioned 1,570 times) and optimized content for learner engagement

• developed bi-monthly performance dashboards for COO, CSO, and divisional VPs; identified trends and recommended targeted interventions for sales reps, trainers, and managers

• partnered with Compliance to assess safety, JCAHO, and regulatory data; identified training trends and gaps to mitigate workplace risks; assessed effectiveness on soft skill training based on NPS ratings provided by callers; and recommended improvements to content and delivery

• built learning analytics and department evaluation strategy using custom mnemonic (Select Evaluation, Tally Data, Analyze Data, Report Results) to train others on evaluation methods

Data Storytelling

• translated data into clear insights that help leaders understand performance opportunities and trends

• transformed learning data into actionable insights by analyzing learner performance, assessment scores, KPIs, and business outcomes (such as sales) to identify trends, measure impact, and guide strategic decisions

• connected training to business performance by correlating learning metrics with quality, productivity, customer satisfaction, compliance, and operational results... demonstrating how workforce capability influences organizational success; for example, the cycle between operations, quality, and training

• built compelling data narratives by combining quantitative analytics with qualitative feedback to explain not just what happened, but why it happened and what actions should be taken to improve performance; the data tells a story of what was done well and what could be improved

Performance Analytics

• designed and implemented performance analytics frameworks to evaluate learning effectiveness by analyzing learner assessments, KPIs, quality metrics, support tickets, and stakeholder feedback... and then translating data into actionable recommendations

• correlated training initiatives with business outcomes by identifying trends in productivity, compliance, customer satisfaction, knowledge retention, and performance metrics to measure ROI and continuously improve workforce capability

• developed executive-ready dashboards and reports to visualize learning effectiveness, identify skill gaps, forecast future training needs, and provide stakeholders with clear, data-driven recommendations for continuous improvement; for example, performed a trend analysis on 90 days sales commision before AND after traiing... determining if the trend indicated coaching was needed for the sales rep, the divisional manager, or the trainer... OR if the content needed to be improved
