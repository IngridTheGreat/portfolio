Human Judgment

Challenging outputs, validating accuracy, identifying potential failures, applying judgment, and knowing when a human needs to make the call

"Human oversight is a critical governance control—not a task to complete when time allows. Human judgment provides the final safeguard that workforce materials are accurate, compliant, and aligned with regulatory requirements.""

Human Judgment

Human Oversight and Validation

• apply human judgment to validate AI-generated and technology-enabled outputs, ensuring information is accurate, relevant, appropriate, and aligned with organizational standards

• review workforce-facing materials for accuracy, clarity, compliance, and regulatory alignment before they are implemented or distributed

• partner with subject matter experts, Compliance, Legal, and business leaders to validate content and decisions where expertise and contextual understanding are critical

• use human oversight as a critical control point, recognizing that technology can generate information, but people remain accountable for determining whether it is right

Context, Ethics and Decision-Making

• evaluate recommendations and outputs within their business context, considering organizational objectives, workforce needs, customer impact, and operational realities

• identify situations where human intervention is required, particularly when decisions involve ambiguity, risk, regulatory requirements, or consequences that technology cannot fully assess

• balance technology-driven efficiency with human expertise, ensuring automation enhances decision-making rather than removing necessary judgment from the process

• apply ethical and practical considerations to workforce solutions, recognizing that effective decisions must work not only technically, but for the people and customers affected by them

Human-in-the-Loop Improvement

• use human feedback to continuously improve processes, technology, training, and AI-enabled solutions, turning real-world experience into actionable improvements

• combine data-driven evaluation with human insight to understand not only what the data indicates, but why performance, adoption, or compliance may be occurring

• establish feedback loops between employees, technology, and business leaders to identify issues, refine solutions, and strengthen adoption

• measure outcomes and intervene when results fall short, using human expertise to determine the appropriate process, technology, training, or behavioral response
