Governance

Governance includes creating the processes, capabilities, and accountability mechanisms that allow people to consistently do the right thing. At the right time. Everytime.

For more than 20 years, I have worked in environments where governance, compliance, accountability, and risk management are critical to organizational performance. Across healthcare, insurance, and military environments, I have helped translate requirements into practical processes, workforce expectations, training, and measurable outcomes.

Governance

Governance and Compliance

• translate regulatory, legal, and organizational requirements into practical processes, procedures, training, job aids, and workforce expectations

• partner with Compliance and Legal teams to review workenablement materials while ensuring content reflected applicable requirements and organizational standards

• integrate compliance into process improvement and change initiatives, helping organizations address risk while creating solutions that employees can easily adopt and execute

• support governance across highly regulated environments, including healthcare and insurance, where regulatory requirements directly influence processes, workforce behavior, and customer outcomes

• connect governance to workforce capability, recognizing that effective compliance depends not only on policies, but on employees understanding what is expected and having the tools and knowledge to execute consistently

Content Governance

• establish structured approaches for managing learning and knowledge content by organizing materials, workflows, documentation, and performance support resources to improve consistency, usability, and accessibility

• apply instructional design standards and quality review processes to ensure training materials, job aids, technical documentation, and knowledge resources are accurate, relevant, and aligned with business and learner needs

• collaborate with stakeholders to maintain content accuracy and relevance by incorporating feedback, updates, and process changes into learning and knowledge assets

• leverage AI-assisted content development workflows to support content creation, refinement, organization, and scalability while maintaining human review and subject matter validation

Evaluation and Accountability

• use data and performance metrics to evaluate compliance, identifying trends and gaps that indicate where processes, training, or workforce practices require improvement

• partner with Compliance and operational leaders to analyze performance data and determine where intervention could reduce risk and improve outcomes

• apply evaluation strategies to identify gaps between expected and actual performance, turning data into actionable recommendations for process improvement and workforce development

• evaluate the effectiveness of training and process changes through learner feedback, assessments, performance metrics, and business outcomes

• connect measurement to continuous improvement, using evidence to determine whether changes were producing the intended behavioral, operational, and compliance results
