Awards and Recognition

Recognition

ROY - Video series, leader huddles, collaboration with Ops and Compliance

Ingrid redesigned the ROY Playbook and process to provide a better agent experience by dividing the content into individual modules and adding leader-led virtual huddles to reinforce learning. The project resulted in six self-paced modules supported by short videos and a presentation guide for review exercises.

She also worked closely with DMS and Sales Integrity, bringing strong collaboration, leadership, and attention to detail to a successful deliverable. Ingrid was instrumental in a TTT series that trained ASU facilitators on video-based learning and feedback practices.

Accessibility

ADA Compliance and Accessibility - Accommodations Made Everyone Happy!

Ingrid worked diligently on the ADA compliance and accessibility project to set standards for the MRSLAD team and share best practices across learning departments. She worked with the team to ensure all content was accessible and compliant with ADA expectations.

Her work was critical as the team developed training resources for all agents, and her presentation at an ATD meeting helped share the work with the broader learning and development community. Survey feedback showed strong appreciation from both employees needing accommodations and those who valued more flexible learning options.

Star Award

Agents got the best experience possible!

I am nominating Ingrid for this STAR award as a result of her work on Certification and Re-certification. Process Improvement

Ticketing system workflow and process improvements

Ingrid’s work on a Change Request Process using MS Teams Planner was a game changer for our team. It simplified editing and revision workflows, eliminated unnecessary email back-and-forth, and created a single location for files and updates.

Reporting also improved, making it easier to track assigned content and upcoming due dates. Her work significantly strengthened team collaboration and process efficiency.
